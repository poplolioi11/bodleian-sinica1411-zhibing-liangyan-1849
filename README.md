# 治病良言(1849;Bodleian Sinica 1411) / Zhi bing liang yan [Good advice on treating illness]: Transcription dataset

## 原書內容與用途 / Historical content

《治病良言》（Zhi bing liang yan）是 1849 年由香港英華書院印刷、末署「黃泥涌仁施醫館謹白」的中文醫館文獻，個人作者未詳。原書陳述基督教施醫宗旨、免費診療及醫療花費，並向病人勸說及早就醫，可供研究十九世紀香港醫療慈善、醫病關係、費用話語與傳教印刷文化。v0.2.1 依 Bodleian Sinica 1411 的 10 幅影像轉錄 12 個原印文字面，提供 TXT、JSON、TEI XML 與官方影像來源對應。文字為 AI 暫定轉錄，未經人類全文雙校或專家審定。

Zhi bing liang yan (治病良言), printed by the Anglo-Chinese College in Hong Kong in 1849, closes with the institutional signature 黃泥涌仁施醫館; its individual author is unknown. The tract presents its Christian charitable purpose and account of free care, addresses medical expense and encourages early attendance, offering evidence for studying medical charity, hospital–patient relations, discourse on expense and missionary printing in nineteenth-century Hong Kong. Version 0.2.1 transcribes 12 original-print text faces across ten images of Bodleian Sinica 1411, providing TXT, JSON and TEI XML with references to the official source images. The text is a provisional AI transcription without human double collation or expert review.
